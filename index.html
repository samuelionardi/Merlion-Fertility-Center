About the centre

Reproductive medicine, and the courage to hope again.

Founded by a team of reproductive endocrinologists and oncofertility specialists, Merlion Fertility Centre was built around a simple belief: fertility care should never feel transactional. Whether you're beginning IVF, preserving fertility before cancer treatment, or navigating unexplained infertility, our clinicians walk alongside you at every appointment, scan, and decision.

We combine internationally accredited laboratory standards with a Singaporean sense of hospitality — private consultation suites, same-week appointments, and a care coordinator assigned to every patient from day one.

“What stayed with us wasn't just the treatment plan — it was that someone remembered our names, our history, and what we'd already been through.” — Patient, Oncofertility Programme

What we treat

Care built around your specific path

Every fertility journey is different. Our services span conception support, fertility preservation, and the diagnostics that help explain what's happening in your body.

IVF & ICSI

In-vitro fertilisation and intracytoplasmic sperm injection, tailored through individualised stimulation protocols and continuous embryo monitoring in our accredited laboratory.

Egg & Embryo Freezing

Vitrification-based freezing for patients planning ahead — whether for career, medical, or personal reasons — with long-term secure cryostorage on site.

Oncofertility

Rapid-turnaround fertility preservation for patients beginning cancer treatment, coordinated directly with your oncology team so no time is lost before therapy begins.

Male Fertility & Andrology

Comprehensive semen analysis, hormonal workups, and surgical sperm retrieval, addressing the male-factor half of the fertility conversation with equal depth.

Recurrent Miscarriage Care

Structured investigation of recurrent pregnancy loss, including immunological, anatomical, and genetic screening, alongside emotional support throughout.

Genetic Testing & Counselling

Pre-implantation genetic testing (PGT) and carrier screening, paired with genetic counselling to help you understand results and next steps clearly.

Our clinicians

Meet the team you'll see, appointment after appointment

Continuity of care matters. You'll work with the same lead clinician throughout your treatment, supported by our embryology and nursing teams.

Medical Director · Reproductive Endocrinology

Dr. Mei Lin Tan

Dr. Tan has led over 3,000 IVF cycles across her 16-year career and specialises in complex cases involving diminished ovarian reserve and repeated implantation failure. She trained in reproductive medicine in London before returning to Singapore to found the centre.

Senior Consultant · Oncofertility

Dr. Rajesh Kumar

Dr. Kumar built the centre's oncofertility programme after seeing how often fertility preservation was overlooked amid cancer diagnoses. He works closely with oncology units across Singapore to fast-track egg, sperm, and tissue preservation before treatment begins.

Consultant · Andrology & Male Fertility

Dr. Sarah Wong

Dr. Wong leads the andrology unit, focusing on male-factor infertility and surgical sperm retrieval techniques. She's known among patients for explaining results in plain language and involving both partners equally in every conversation.
